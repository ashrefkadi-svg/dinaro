# DINARO+ Financial Academy — source register

This register lists primary or established educational references used to guide the curriculum. It is not a substitute for checking current laws, rates, or product terms.

## General financial capability
- OECD/INFE — financial literacy and inclusion: https://www.oecd.org/financial/education/
- Consumer Financial Protection Bureau — Your Money, Your Goals and consumer tools: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tools/your-money-your-goals/
- World Bank — financial inclusion and consumer protection resources: https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/financialinclusion

## Saving, budgeting, cash flow and debt
- CFPB — budgeting and savings resources: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/

## Editorial rules
1. Prefer official primary sources for Algerian tax, banking, consumer, and regulatory statements.
2. Record the publication date and review date for content that can change.
3. Distinguish general principles from jurisdiction-specific requirements.
4. Never present an illustrative calculation as a guaranteed outcome.
5. Add a qualified professional review for operational legal, tax, or investment guidance.
